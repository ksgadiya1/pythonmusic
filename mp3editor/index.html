Frequently asked questions

How do I cut an MP3 online for free?

Upload your MP3, enter a start and end time (e.g. 0:30 to 1:15), click "Add Trimmed Piece", then download. No signup needed.

Can I merge multiple MP3 files online?

Yes. Add as many trimmed pieces as you like, reorder them with the arrow buttons, then click "Merge Pieces" to stitch them into one file.

Does this MP3 editor reduce audio quality?

No. Trimming is a lossless PCM operation. The export automatically matches your original file's bitrate, sample rate, and channel count.

What is crossfade and how do I use it?

Crossfade smoothly blends the end of one clip into the start of the next instead of a hard cut. Use the crossfade slider (0–2000 ms) before merging.

What audio formats are supported?

MP3, WAV, and M4A files are supported for upload. The output is always a high-quality MP3.

Is my audio file uploaded to a server?

Your file is only used within your browser session for processing and is not permanently stored or shared.
